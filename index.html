About Me

I'm passionate about cybersecurity. My focus is on reverse engineering, offensive security, hardware hacking, steganography, and forensics. I spend most of my time self-studying and trying to improve continuously, and I'm especially drawn to the offensive side, actively reading and experimenting with evasion techniques. I find and report vulnerabilities in real software for the fun of it.

I'm a Linux kernel contributor with several CVEs in mainline. I've found and reported vulnerabilities in open-source projects (VLC, libmspack, file(1), onnxruntime-extensions, gdk-pixbuf). I did malware analysis research at the TU Delft Cyber Security Research Group, which led to a publication at ACM IMC 2026: "Investigating Next.js React2Shell Exploitation."

On the CTF side, I founded the vianu_hack CTF team (peaked at #2 nationally on CTFtime) and currently compete as part of both it and TU Delft's CTF team. I've authored challenges for national and international competitions, and mentored at Unbreakable Romania, running workshops on reverse engineering Android and iOS applications.

I'm also deeply into AI, especially Image Recognition & Natural Language Processing, and I love solving algorithmic and logical problems. I attended Jane Street FTTP (Software Engineering Track) in London, a notoriously selective program for first-year students in software engineering and quantitative finance. Back in high school, I founded both the cybersecurity and AI clubs at Tudor Vianu National High School of Computer Science in Bucharest.

When I'm off-keyboard, you'll find me travelling, shooting photos, hiking, skiing, biking, swimming, or spending time with my friends.
